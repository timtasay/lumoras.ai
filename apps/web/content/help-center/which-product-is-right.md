The right Lumoras product depends on your industry. Salons and spas use Sonorch, restaurants use SeasonX, other service businesses use Lumoras POS with Lumoras Voice, and retail stores use Lumoras Sound. Diners find restaurants on KitchenSpot.

## What this does

Every product runs on the same core, so the AI receptionist, the register and your customer records work together. The difference is the vocabulary and the workflow each product is built around. A salon books a balayage with a named stylist. A restaurant seats a party of four at 7:30. An auto shop holds a drop-off bay.

## Pick your product

1. **Salons, barbers, spas, nail and lash studios, med spas: [Sonorch](https://sonorch.ai).** An AI receptionist and POS that answers every call, books and reschedules appointments, handles deposits and no-show policies, and checks clients out at the chair.
2. **Restaurants, cafés, bars, pizzerias and takeout kitchens: [SeasonX](https://seasonx.ai).** An AI receptionist that takes reservations and phone orders, plus a POS with a live floor view and a kitchen display.
3. **Clinics, dental, auto service, home services, fitness, pet grooming, tutoring and repair shops: Lumoras POS with Lumoras Voice.** The POS handles appointments or tickets, payments, staff, inventory and multiple locations. Lumoras Voice adds the AI receptionist on the same calendar whenever you want it.
4. **Retail stores: Lumoras Sound.** Zoned in-store music, playlists that change with the time of day, scheduled announcements and voice paging, managed across locations from one console.
5. **Diners looking for somewhere to eat: [KitchenSpot](https://kitchenspot.ai).** Restaurant discovery with full menus, dish-by-dish ratings from verified diners and pickup ordering. It connects to SeasonX.

## Check that it fits

Ask yourself three questions before you sign up:

- **What do customers book?** Appointments with a person point to Sonorch or Lumoras POS. Tables and orders point to SeasonX.
- **Where do calls go today?** If calls go unanswered while staff are busy, plan to switch on the receptionist early.
- **Do you run more than one location?** Lumoras POS and Lumoras Sound both roll locations up into one view.

## If you fit more than one

- **A med spa** can use Sonorch, since it is built for appointment-based beauty and wellness.
- **A café with retail shelves** can use SeasonX at the counter and talk to us about Lumoras Sound for the floor.
- **A business that is not listed** can start on Lumoras POS. It is configured for your services during setup.
