To schedule a store announcement, create it under **Announcements**, write the message, choose the zones that should hear it, and set when it plays. Announcements can repeat on a schedule, such as a closing reminder every night, or play once. Voice paging lets staff speak to one zone or the whole store when needed.

## What this does

Announcements take routine messages off your team's to-do list. The closing reminder plays on time every night, curbside pickups get called at the door, and promotions run in the zones where they make sense. Every location can share the same announcements from one console, with local times applied.

## Create an announcement

1. Open **Sound**, then **Announcements**.
2. Choose a type, such as store closing, curbside ready or promotion.
3. Write the message. Keep it short and say the most important part first.
4. Preview how it sounds and adjust the wording until it reads clearly out loud.
5. Choose the zones that should play it. A curbside message belongs at the front, while a promotion might play on the main floor only.
6. Save.

## Set the schedule

1. Open the announcement and choose when it plays.
2. Pick the days and time, or a repeat, such as every 20 minutes during a sale.
3. For closing messages, schedule them a set time before you close, for example 15 minutes and 5 minutes ahead.
4. Save. The announcement now runs on its own.

Example: a store that closes at 9 PM and wants two reminders sets one for 8:45 PM and one for 8:55 PM.

## Page a zone

Use voice paging when something needs a person now, such as a call for help at the register. Choose the zone, or the whole store, then speak your message. Keep pages short so the music can carry on.

## Check that it worked

1. Play the announcement once from the console and listen in each chosen zone.
2. Confirm zones you did not choose stayed quiet.
3. Check the schedule list shows the next time it will play.
4. After the first scheduled play, check that it went out at the right local time.

## Troubleshooting

- **The announcement is hard to hear.** Raise the announcement level for that zone, or shorten the message.
- **It played in the wrong place.** Review the zones selected on the announcement.
- **It played at the wrong time.** Check the store's time zone and your closing hours.
- **Customers say it plays too often.** Lengthen the repeat interval or limit it to busy hours.
