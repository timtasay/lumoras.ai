BHow it worksm. 02

From first call to full orchestra in four movements.

No rip-and-replace. Bring what you run today, tune it to sound like you, go live on your existing number and conduct everything from one place.

01 / 04 · Connect
  1. 01Connect

    Connect

    Bring what you already run.

    Import your POS data, calendar, menu or service list, or start fresh on Lumoras POS. Staff, hours, prices and policies come across once and stay in sync.

    • POS and calendar import
    • Menus and service lists
    • Or start on Lumoras POS
  2. 02Tune

    Tune

    Make it sound like you.

    Set the voice and the greeting, your deposit and cancellation policies, hours and escalation rules. For stores, compose the soundscape: zones, dayparts, announcements.

    • Voice and greeting
    • Policies and escalation
    • Store soundscapes
  3. 03Go live

    Go live

    Forward a number. Pair a speaker.

    Point your existing line at Lumoras and pair your store speakers. Calls are answered on the first ring, music starts on schedule, and your phone number never changes.

    • Keep your number
    • Speaker pairing
    • Staff escalation paths
  4. 04Orchestrate

    Orchestrate

    Conduct it all from one console.

    Every call, sale and sound lands in one console with live analytics, across every location. See what was booked, what was sold and what played, then adjust the score.

    • Live analytics
    • Every location
    • Roles and audit trail
